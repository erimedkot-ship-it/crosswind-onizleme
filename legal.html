Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how Crosswind Aviation LTD (“Crosswind”, “we”) handles personal data shared through this website and in our communication with you.

Who we are

Crosswind Aviation LTD, Gazimagusa Freezone Area, PO Box 909, Cyprus. We arrange aviation fuel supply and, as a broker, charter flights. For privacy questions, write to sales@cwavia.com (fuel) or charter@cwavia.com (charter).

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, company, email address and phone or WhatsApp number.
  • Request details, such as the airport, date, aircraft, route and number of passengers, and our correspondence with you.
  • For a charter flight you decide to go ahead with, the passenger details needed to arrange it.

We do not ask for payment details through this website.

How the quote form works

When you send the quote form, the website server delivers your details by email to the team concerned (fuel or charter); the website does not keep a separate copy. To protect the form against misuse, the server uses technical information such as your IP address for up to 10 minutes; this information is not added to your request. Our web server also keeps standard access logs, including IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting. If sending is not possible, the form prepares an email or WhatsApp message on your device, which you send to us yourself.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and prepare a quote.
  • To plan and arrange the fuel supply or flight you ask for.
  • To stay in contact about your request.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

Only as far as your request requires: fuel suppliers, into-plane and handling agents for fuel; the commercial operator of a charter flight and the service providers involved in it; our communication and IT providers (for example email, WhatsApp and website hosting); only if you consent to analytics cookies, our analytics provider Google; and authorities legally entitled to receive it, for example for flight permits, customs and passport control. Some of them are located outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area; transfers are made for the purposes above and as permitted by applicable law. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

For as long as your request and our business relationship require, and for the periods set by law. Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Cookies and local storage

Essential local storage remembers your cookie choice; it is needed for the website to work and needs no consent.

Only if you select “Accept”, we use Google Analytics (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC) to understand how the website is used, for example which pages are visited and how many quote requests are sent. Google Analytics sets cookies named _ga and _ga_*, which expire after 13 months at the latest. The data may be processed by Google outside Türkiye, including in the United States. Advertising features are switched off, and we do not use advertising cookies. If you select “Reject”, Google Analytics is not loaded.

You can change your choice at any time under “Cookie Preferences” at the bottom of the Home, Fuel, Charter and About pages; when you withdraw consent, the analytics cookies are deleted. We ask for your choice again after 12 months. Fonts and images are served from this website itself.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, or object to its processing, by writing to the addresses above. If you are in Türkiye, our KVKK Notice explains your rights under Law No. 6698.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the current version.

KVKK Notice

Last updated: November 2026

Information notice under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”).

Data controller

Crosswind Aviation LTD, Gazimagusa Freezone Area, PO Box 909, Cyprus · sales@cwavia.com (fuel) · charter@cwavia.com (charter)

Personal data processed

Identity data (name, if given); contact data (email address, phone or WhatsApp number); customer transaction data (request details such as airport, date, aircraft, route and number of passengers, and correspondence); transaction security data (IP address, used by the server for up to 10 minutes to protect the quote form against misuse, and standard web server access logs); for a charter flight you decide to go ahead with, the passenger data the flight requires, such as passengers’ names, nationality, date of birth and passport or ID details; and, only with your explicit consent to analytics cookies, website usage data collected by Google Analytics (pages visited, device and browser information, approximate location).

Purposes

  • Fuel requests (fuel team): preparing quotes, arranging the uplift with suppliers and handling agents, and invoicing.
  • Charter requests (charter team): preparing quotes, arranging the flight with the commercial operator, and passing on the passenger details the flight requires.
  • For both: communicating with you about your request and meeting legal and financial obligations.
  • Website security: protecting the quote form against spam and misuse.
  • Website analytics, only with your explicit consent: understanding how the website is used and improving it.

Legal grounds

Replying to requests, preparing quotes and arranging fuel or flights: processing being directly related to the establishment or performance of a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c). Legal, tax and accounting obligations and information required by authorities: compliance with legal obligations (Art. 5/2-ç). For a charter flight, notifying passenger details to the authorities of the countries concerned for flight permits, customs and passport control: compliance with legal obligations (Art. 5/2-ç) and performance of the contract (Art. 5/2-c). Website security: our legitimate interest in protecting the quote form against misuse (Art. 5/2-f). Website analytics: your explicit consent (Art. 5/1), which you can withdraw at any time under “Cookie Preferences”.

Transfers

As far as your request requires, to fuel suppliers, into-plane and handling agents; to the commercial operator of a charter flight and the service providers involved in it; to our communication and IT service providers (email, WhatsApp, website hosting); with your explicit consent, to Google as our analytics service provider; and to authorities legally entitled to receive it (for example for flight permits, customs and passport control). Because the service is often provided at airports abroad, some of these recipients are located outside Türkiye, and your data is shared with them only as far as your request requires. Transfers abroad are made in accordance with Article 9 of the KVKK.

Retention

We keep personal data for as long as your request and our business relationship require and for the retention periods set by law; after that it is deleted, destroyed or anonymised. Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Method of collection

Electronically and verbally: through the quote form on this website, whose details reach our team by email, or messages prepared with it and sent by you; by email, WhatsApp and phone; automatically, through the technical information the server uses to protect the form (such as IP address); and, only with your consent, through analytics cookies. Data may come directly from you or from the person or company making the request on your behalf, for example a trip support company or the person booking a charter flight for a group.

Your rights (Article 11)

You may ask whether your personal data is processed and request information about it; learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred; request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; request deletion or destruction under Article 7; request that third parties to whom data was transferred be notified of such correction or deletion; object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

How to apply

You can send a request about your rights, in Turkish, as a signed letter to the postal address above; or, signed with a secure electronic or mobile signature or sent from the email address you have previously given us and that is in our records, by email to the addresses above.

Your request should include your name and surname, your signature for written requests, your Turkish ID number (for foreign nationals, nationality and passport number), your address for notifications, your email address and phone number if any, and the subject of your request.

Requests are concluded free of charge as soon as possible and within 30 days at the latest, depending on their nature. If the process involves an additional cost, a fee under the tariff set by the Personal Data Protection Board may be charged.

If your request is refused, you find the reply insufficient or no reply is given in time, you may lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board within 30 days of learning of the reply, and in any case within 60 days of the date of your request.
