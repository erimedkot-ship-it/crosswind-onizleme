How a Charter Broker Works

We do not own or operate aircraft. Charter flights are operated by commercial operators holding the air operator certificate (AOC) the flight requires; as a broker, we arrange your flight with one of them. This lets us compare options from different operators for your route.

What We Do

  • Understand your trip and what matters to you
  • Source and compare suitable aircraft from commercial operators
  • Present the options and a written quote
  • Remain your point of contact for the trip

What the Operator Does

  • Operates the flight under its air operator certificate (AOC)
  • Provides the aircraft and the crew
  • Is responsible for the operation of the flight

Charter Questions

What travellers and organisers ask us first. For anything else, our charter team is a message away.

Contact Operations
Do you own or operate the aircraft?

No. We are a charter broker. Charter flights are operated by commercial operators holding the air operator certificate (AOC) the flight requires, and we arrange your flight with one of them.

What do you need to start a request?

The route, the date, the number of passengers and where we can reach you. That is all the form asks for.

What else should we mention?

Preferred times, luggage (especially large or special items) and any special requests. Our team will ask if anything else matters for your flight.

Can you arrange flights at short notice?

Short-notice requests are welcome. What is possible depends on the aircraft and operators available at that time, and we tell you the options.

Do you arrange cargo flights too?

Yes. For time-critical, palletised or outsize shipments, we source the right freighter aircraft from commercial operators and coordinate the flight end to end.
