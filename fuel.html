Where We Supply Fuel

In Türkiye, we support aircraft departing on international flights. Across all five regions, availability varies by airport and date, so we check it for each request.

Türkiye

Our home base at the crossroads of Europe, the Gulf and Central Asia. Direct coordination with local physical suppliers across major hubs and coastal airports for international departures.

Operational focus: Istanbul and Ankara, the Black Sea coast and the summer coastal airports of Antalya, Bodrum and Dalaman.

Europe

Year-round supply across financial capitals and executive hubs in the UK, Western Europe and the Mediterranean, with more than one supplier at many airports in peak season.

Operational focus: slot-coordinated airports, Mediterranean summer traffic and winter alpine routes.

Middle East

Jet A-1 supply across key Gulf and Red Sea hubs connecting Europe, Asia and Africa, for both executive flights and wide-body cargo operations.

Operational focus: 24/7 turnaround support, tech-stop uplifts and high-demand winter schedules.

Central Asia

Strategic tech-stop and destination airports across Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Azerbaijan and the wider Caspian–Central Asian corridor linking Europe and East Asia.

Operational focus: long-sector fuel planning, cargo freighter uplifts and local handling coordination.

Africa

Supply across North, West, East and Central African gateways—from Mediterranean hubs in Libya, Egypt and Morocco to sub-Saharan capitals.

Operational focus: advance fuel confirmation and, where available, alternative suppliers on long-range sectors.

Beyond Our Core Regions

Operating further afield? We also arrange fuel uplifts across the Americas and the Caribbean (including the US, Dominican Republic and Brazil), the Indian Ocean (Maldives, Mauritius) and Asia-Pacific (China, South Korea, Thailand, Indonesia and India).

Fuel Questions

What operators and flight planners ask us most. For anything else, our fuel team is a message away.

Contact Operations
Do you supply fuel for domestic flights in Türkiye?

In Türkiye, we support aircraft departing on international flights. Across Europe, the Middle East, Central Asia and Africa, we can generally support domestic and international flights alike.

What do you need for a quote?

The airport, the date, the aircraft and where we can reach you. If you have them, the flight type, flight schedule, estimated uplift, handling agent and billing company help us reply faster.

Can you help with short-notice requests?

Yes, send them through; operations run 24/7. What is possible at short notice depends on the airport and the time, and we tell you the available options.

Do we need a fixed schedule for regular supply?

No. A regular supply arrangement can be set up around the airports you use most, with or without a fixed timetable. Ad hoc requests are just as welcome.

Which aircraft do you supply?

We supply Jet A-1 for turbine aircraft, from business jets and turboprops to narrow-body and wide-body airliners and cargo freighters. Share your aircraft type and estimated uplift, and our team will check availability for your airport and date.
